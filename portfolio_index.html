How I work

The catalog lifecycle I run for every product, from first vendor contact to a listing that keeps selling. Scroll to follow one product through it.

  1. Step 1

    Onboard the vendor

    Collect product, cost and delivery data; validate COGS and margins against gross-margin targets; benchmark competitor pricing before anything goes live.

  2. Step 2

    Upload and map attributes

    Load products into Magento 2, Shopify or WooCommerce with clean taxonomy and attribute mapping, so filters, search and feeds work from day one.

  3. Step 3

    Run pre-publish QA

    Check images, titles, descriptions and specifications against written SOPs and content standards. Errors are caught here, not by customers.

  4. Step 4

    Launch across storefronts

    Publish consistently across all 8 stores with the same brand standards and messaging, coordinating with Marketing, Merchandising and Warehouse teams.

  5. Step 5

    Keep the feed healthy

    Monitor Google Merchant Center daily, resolve disapprovals and policy flags at the root cause, and report account health.

  6. Step 6

    Optimise and repeat

    Improve PDP content for discoverability and conversion, track performance in dashboards, and use AI-assisted checks to make the next cycle faster.
